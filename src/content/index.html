Design-system tooling

I built two Claude Code skills: one to review and fix UI components and documentation against internal standards, improving quality and reducing manual effort; another generates high-fidelity Figma prototypes from design-system components, reducing prototype time from hours to minutes.

I want technology to feel like a natural extension of us.

That’s what draws me to neural interfaces: the possibility of interacting with technology with as little friction as possible.

In an earlier project, we brought together speech datasets covering different accents and trained machine-learning and deep-learning systems to predict emotion across accents.

I’m brushing up on data science through projects in EEG-based intent classification and imagined-speech decoding. I want to get a feel for what these signals can tell us, and what people might actually find useful.

I’m also taking part in the EEG/EMG Foundation Challenge at the NeurIPS 2026 Brain & Body Workshop.
